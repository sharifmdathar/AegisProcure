# Aegis Procure — Brand Brief

---

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Aegis Procure
**Tagline:** *Trustless Procurement. Zero Bid Leakage.*
**Category:** ZK Infrastructure / GovTech / B2B Procurement

---

## Mission

To eliminate corruption, bid-shading, and information asymmetry from public and enterprise procurement by making sealed-bid auctions mathematically verifiable and permanently private.

---

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|-----------|
| **Trustworthy** | Clean, minimal UI. No dark patterns. Privacy by default. |
| **Technical** | Precise language. ZK terminology explained, not hidden. |
| **Authoritative** | Confident copy. We don't hedge on privacy guarantees. |
| **Modern** | Dark theme, purple accent, monospace code elements. |

### Typography

- **Headings:** Inter, font-weight 900 (black), tight tracking
- **Body:** Inter, font-weight 400, relaxed line-height
- **Code / addresses:** JetBrains Mono or Fira Code
- **UI labels:** Inter, font-weight 500, uppercase + wide tracking for metadata

### Logo Concept

A shield (🔒) combined with a lock — representing the "Aegis" (divine shield) protecting bid data. The lock is open only for the winner; all others remain sealed.

---

## Voice & Tone

### Headlines
- Short, declarative, confident
- Lead with the privacy guarantee
- Examples:
  - *"Trustless Procurement. Zero Bid Leakage."*
  - *"Start in the dark. Ship in the light."*
  - *"Mathematical certainty for sealed-bid auctions."*

### Body copy
- Technical but accessible
- Explain ZK concepts in plain language alongside precise terminology
- Never use vague words like "secure" without explaining the mechanism
- Example: *"Your bid amount flows through ZK circuit witnesses only — it never touches the public ledger."*

### Error messages
- Specific and actionable
- Example: *"Hash mismatch: the salt or bid amount you entered doesn't match your commitment. Check your saved salt."*

---

## Target Audience

### Primary — Government Procurement Officers
- Pain point: Bid-rigging, information leaks, legal liability
- Message: *"Aegis Procure gives you a legally defensible, tamper-proof audit trail with zero bid exposure."*

### Secondary — Enterprise Procurement Teams
- Pain point: Supplier collusion, internal leaks, manual processes
- Message: *"Automate sealed-bid procurement with cryptographic guarantees. No spreadsheets. No leaks."*

### Tertiary — Web3 / Midnight Ecosystem Developers
- Pain point: Finding real-world ZK use cases
- Message: *"The reference implementation for ZK commit-reveal auctions on Midnight."*

## Competitive Positioning

| Feature | Aegis Procure | Traditional e-Procurement | Generic Blockchain Auction |
|---------|--------------|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Bid privacy | ✅ ZK proofs | ❌ Admin can see all bids | ❌ All bids public on-chain |
| Trustless | ✅ Smart contract | ❌ Relies on vendor | ⚠️ Partial |
| Audit trail | ✅ On-chain | ⚠️ Database logs | ✅ On-chain |
| Losing bid privacy | ✅ Permanent | ❌ Stored in DB | ❌ Public forever |

---

## Key Messages

1. **Privacy by math, not policy** — ZK proofs enforce bid privacy at the protocol level. No admin can leak your bid.
2. **Selective disclosure** — Only the winner is revealed. Everyone else stays dark.
3. **Built for compliance** — Immutable on-chain audit trail satisfies procurement regulations.
4. **Open source** — Fully auditable. No black boxes.
